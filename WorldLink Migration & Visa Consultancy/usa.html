Why Study in the USA?

The USA is the #1 destination for international students globally. Here's why thousands choose America every year.

🎓 World-Ranked Universities

The USA has more universities in the global top 100 than any other country. Degrees are recognized and valued by employers worldwide.

💼 OPT & Work Opportunities

F-1 student visa holders can work on-campus and apply for Optional Practical Training (OPT) — up to 12 months of work experience after graduation. STEM graduates can extend OPT for up to 24 months.

🔬 Research & Innovation

American universities lead global research in AI, biotechnology, medicine, engineering, and space science. World-class labs and abundant research funding.

🌎 Diversity of Programs

From Ivy League business schools to state universities offering vocational training — the USA has a program for every academic and career goal.

🏙️ Campus Life & Culture

Experience diverse campus communities, fraternities and sororities, NCAA sports, clubs, and a vibrant social scene in cities and college towns across 50 states.

📊 Generous Scholarships

American universities offer merit-based and need-based scholarships for international students. Many provide full tuition waivers for outstanding academic profiles.

F-1 Student Visa Process

1

Get Accepted by a SEVP-Approved University

Apply to universities and receive your I-20 form (Certificate of Eligibility) from the school after being accepted.

2

Pay the SEVIS Fee

Pay the I-901 SEVIS fee ($350) to the US Immigration & Customs Enforcement website before your visa interview.

3

Complete the DS-160 Form

Fill out the Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application (DS-160) on the US Embassy website. Keep your confirmation number.

4

Schedule Your Visa Interview

Book an appointment at the US Embassy in Dhaka. Interview wait times vary — book early!

5

Attend the Visa Interview

Bring all required documents. Be honest, confident, and clear about your intent to study and return home.

📋 Required Documents for F-1 Visa

  • Valid passport (min. 6 months)
  • DS-160 confirmation page
  • I-20 form from your university
  • SEVIS fee receipt
  • Visa application fee receipt
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • English test scores (IELTS/TOEFL)
  • Proof of financial support ($20,000–$50,000/yr)
  • Interview appointment letter

✅ Proof of Financial Support

You must demonstrate ability to pay tuition + living expenses. Accepted documents: bank statements, scholarship letters, sponsor affidavits, or a combination thereof.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

🎓 University Scholarships

  • Harvard Financial Aid: Need-blind, avg. aid $60K/year
  • MIT Scholarships: Full need-based aid, no loans
  • Fulbright Program: Full scholarships for graduate students
  • HubSpot Scholarships: $5K–$20K for CS students
  • University-specific merit scholarships: Up to full tuition waiver

🏦 External Scholarships

  • Aga Khan Foundation: For Muslim students from developing countries
  • Joint Japan/World Bank: Graduate scholarships for developing nations
  • Chevening (UK govt): For graduate study in the US
  • Inlaks Shivderi: For Indian students (subset can apply to US)
  • Commonwealth Scholarship: For Commonwealth country nationals
