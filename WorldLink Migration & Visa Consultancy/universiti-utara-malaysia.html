About Universiti Utara Malaysia

Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM) was established in 1984 as the Northern University of Malaysia. It is located in Sintok, Kedah — a serene region in the northern part of Peninsular Malaysia, near the border with Perlis and Thailand. The campus sits on a 1,061-hectare site surrounded by hills and natural forest, providing a calm and conducive environment for academic pursuit.

UUM is the premier university in Malaysia specializing in business, management, economics, law, and social sciences. Its AACSB, AMBA, and EQUIS-accredited business school ranks among the top business schools in Asia. The university also has strong programs in information technology, education, and creative arts.

With over 30,000 students and 3,000+ international students from more than 60 countries, UUM offers a truly global learning experience. The medium of instruction is English, and the university is committed to producing graduates who are not only academically proficient but also socially responsible and globally competitive.

Scholarships Available

UUM and the Malaysian government offer scholarships for outstanding international students:

🎓 Malaysian Government Scholarships

  • Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) — Full tuition + living allowance + flights for postgraduate students
  • IsDB Scholarship — For students from Islamic Development Bank member countries
  • Commonwealth Scholarship — For students from Commonwealth nations

🏫 UUM Internal Scholarships

  • UUM Graduate Scholarship — Tuition waiver + monthly stipend for research-based postgraduate students
  • Dean’s List Achievement Award — Cash prize for top-achieving undergraduate students
  • Part-time research assistant & teaching assistant positions — Paid academic work while studying

Admission Requirements

Requirements vary by program. The general requirements for international students are listed below.

📋 General Requirements

  • 🎓
    Bachelor's Degree: Completion of 12 years of education OR A-Levels/Foundation with minimum GPA 2.0–3.0 depending on program.
  • 📚
    Master's Degree: Bachelor's degree in relevant field with minimum CGPA 2.50 OR equivalent. Some coursework programs have flexible entry.
  • 🔬
    PhD: Master's degree in relevant field. Research proposal required. Publications preferred for competitive candidates.
  • 🌐
    English Proficiency: IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 550 / MUET Band 4 or equivalent (waived for native English speakers).

Campus Life & Facilities

🏠 Accommodation

On-campus apartments and residential colleges with basic amenities. The UUM residential campus can accommodate over 10,000 students. Nearby off-campus options in Sintok and Alor Setar are affordable.

🍽️ Dining & Social Life

Multiple food courts and restaurants serving Malaysian and international cuisine. The campus has cultural clubs, religious societies, sports teams, and a vibrant arts scene.

🏋️ Sports & Recreation

Olympic swimming pool, sports complex, gym, running tracks, football pitches, basketball and badminton courts. UUM is known for its strong sports program.

📚 Library & Learning Centre

The main library (Perpustakaan Sultan Abdul Halім) holds over 1 million books, digital journals, and databases. 24/7 study rooms available during exam periods.

🏛️ UUM Innovation Park & Business Centre

Entrepreneurship hub for student startups. Incubation centre, business clinic, and networking events with Malaysian industries and multinational companies.

🚌 Location & Transport

Located in Sintok, Kedah — close to the Malaysia-Thailand border. Free campus shuttles. 45 minutes to Alor Setar city. Near Perlis border. Access to KL via express bus or train.
