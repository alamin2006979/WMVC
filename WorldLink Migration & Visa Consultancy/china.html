Why Study in China?

China is now the 3rd most popular destination for international students globally. Here's why thousands are choosing China for their education.

🏆 World-Class Universities

Tsinghua University ranks #15 globally and Peking University (#18). China now has 7 universities in the QS top 100. Degrees are internationally accredited and recognized globally.

💰 Extremely Affordable

Tuition fees range from ¥15,000–35,000/year (~$2,100–$4,900). Living costs in Chinese cities are very affordable — food, transport, and accommodation cost far less than in the UK or Australia.

🎓 CSC Full Scholarships

The Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) covers full tuition, accommodation, living allowance (¥3,000/month), and health insurance. This makes studying in China essentially free!

🔬 Cutting-Edge Research

China is the world's #1 publisher of scientific research. In fields like AI, 5G technology, renewable energy, quantum computing, and biotechnology, China leads globally.

🌐 Learn Mandarin — a Global Language

China has the world's largest economy and is a major player in global business. Learning Mandarin gives you a massive career advantage in the global job market.

🌏 Strategic Gateway to Asia

China's Belt and Road initiative and growing economic influence make it a strategic hub. A degree from China opens doors to careers across Asia and globally.

X1 Student Visa (Visa for Long-term Study) Process

1

Apply & Receive JW201 / JW202 Form

Apply to a Chinese university and receive the Form JW201 (for scholarship students) or JW202 (for self-funded students). This is your admission document.

2

Apply for Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) — Optional

If applying for CSC scholarship, submit your application through the Chinese Embassy or directly via the CSC website. Results typically come within 2 months.

3

Submit Visa Application at Chinese Embassy

Apply for the X1 visa (valid for long-term study) at the Chinese Embassy in Dhaka. Bring: JW201/JW202 form, passport, health examination certificate, photo.

4

Health Examination in Bangladesh

Complete a health examination at an approved hospital and obtain a health certificate. This is required for the X1 visa.

5

Register with University & Get Residence Permit

Within 30 days of arrival in China, register at your university and apply for a Residence Permit — which allows you to stay for the full duration of your studies.

📋 X1 Visa Requirements

  • JW201 or JW202 form from your Chinese university
  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Health examination certificate
  • Passport-size photograph (white background)
  • Completed visa application form
  • Proof of accommodation in China
  • Financial proof (for self-funded students)
  • CSC scholarship certificate (if applicable)

✅ Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

CSC covers: Full tuition waiver, on-campus accommodation, living allowance (¥3,000/month for doctoral, ¥2,500 for master's), health insurance. WorldLink helps with CSC application — one of the most generous scholarships in the world.

Popular Programs for International Students

Chinese universities excel in STEM fields, medicine, and business — with many programs taught in English for international students.

💻 Computer Science & AI

CS, AI, Machine Learning, Data Science. China's tech industry (Alibaba, Tencent, Huawei) is world-leading. Research opportunities in 5G, cloud computing, and robotics.

⚙️ Engineering

Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Civil Engineering. China leads in high-speed rail, bridges, and infrastructure. Strong research funding.

🏥 Medicine

MBBS (taught in English), TCM (Traditional Chinese Medicine), Pharmacy, Public Health. Peking, Fudan, and Tsinghua have top medical schools.

🌐 Mandarin Chinese Language

Chinese language programs (HSK preparation) — highly valuable for future career prospects. Many scholarships include 1 year of language training.

📈 Business & Economics

MBA, International Business, Economics. Programs taught in English at top schools. Access to China's booming economy and financial sector.

🔬 Biotechnology & Life Sciences

Genetics, Pharmacology, Environmental Science, Marine Biology. China's research output in life sciences is growing rapidly.
