Why Study in Malaysia?

Malaysia has rapidly become one of Asia's most popular study destinations — offering quality education at a fraction of the cost of Western universities.

💰 Affordable World-Class Education

Tuition fees at Malaysian universities are significantly lower than in the UK, USA, or Australia — while the quality of education meets international standards. Full degree programs available from MYR 15,000/year.

🌐 English as the Medium of Instruction

Unlike many non-English-speaking countries, Malaysian universities teach primarily in English. This makes it accessible for Bangladeshi students without needing to learn a new language.

🏆 Internationally Recognized Degrees

Malaysian universities have partnerships with UK, US, Australian, and Canadian universities. You can earn a degree from a foreign university without leaving Malaysia — at a fraction of the cost.

🌏 Diverse & Welcoming Culture

Malaysia is a multicultural nation where Malay, Chinese, Indian, and indigenous communities live together harmoniously. International students feel welcomed and at home quickly.

🗺️ Strategic Location

Located close to Bangladesh, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia. Frequent flights, similar food culture, and easy travel make it convenient for Bangladeshi students.

💼 Post-Study Opportunities

Malaysia offers a Post-Study Work Visa for international graduates. The country is also a growing hub for multinational companies in Southeast Asia.

Malaysia Student Pass (Visa) Process

1

Receive Offer Letter & EMGS Approval

Apply to a Malaysian university and receive your offer letter. The university will apply for Electronic Migration Services (EMGS) approval on your behalf.

2

Pay EMGS Fees & Health Insurance

Pay the EMGS processing fee and mandatory health insurance (OSHIC). This is required before your visa can be issued.

3

Receive Your Student Pass (Visa)

Once EMGS approves, your passport will be stamped with the Student Pass (valist pass). Your university will guide you on pickup or delivery.

4

Medical Check-Up in Bangladesh

Complete a medical examination at an approved panel clinic before traveling. Results are submitted to EMGS.

5

Travel & Orientation

Travel to Malaysia and complete university orientation. Register for your semester and collect your student ID card.

📋 Malaysia Student Pass Requirements

  • Offer letter from a Malaysian university
  • EMGS approval letter (processed by university)
  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Medical examination report
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHIC)
  • Proof of funds for living expenses
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Visa fee payment
