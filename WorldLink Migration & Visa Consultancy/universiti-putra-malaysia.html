About Universiti Putra Malaysia

Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) is a premier research university located in Serdang, Selangor, Malaysia. Originally founded as the School of Agriculture in 1931, UPM has grown into a world-class institution with a strong reputation in agriculture, forestry, veterinary sciences, food science, biotechnology, and environmental sciences.

The university is recognized internationally, ranking among the top 1% of universities globally (QS World University Rankings). UPM's serene 5.6 square kilometer campus features state-of-the-art facilities, research centers, student accommodation, and extensive green spaces.

UPM welcomes over 5,000 international students from more than 70 countries, making it one of the most diverse universities in Malaysia. The medium of instruction is English, making it an ideal destination for international students.

Scholarships Available

UPM and the Malaysian government offer several scholarships for outstanding international students:

🎓 Malaysian Government Scholarships

  • Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) — Full tuition + living allowance + flights for postgraduate students
  • Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) Scholarship — For students from IsDB member countries
  • Commonwealth Scholarship — For students from Commonwealth nations

🏫 UPM Internal Scholarships

  • UPM Graduate Research Scholarship — Tuition waiver + monthly stipend for PhD students
  • Faculty-specific grants — Available for high-achieving undergraduate students
  • Part-time research assistant positions — Paid research work while studying

Admission Requirements

Requirements vary by program. The general requirements for international students are listed below.

📋 General Requirements

  • 🎓
    Bachelor's Degree: Completion of 12 years of education OR A-Levels/Foundation with minimum GPA 3.0. Specific subject requirements apply.
  • 📚
    Master's Degree: Bachelor's degree in relevant field with minimum CGPA 2.75 OR equivalent. Some programs require research proposal.
  • 🔬
    PhD: Master's degree in relevant field with publications OR a master's by research. English proficiency + research proposal required.
  • 🌐
    English Proficiency: IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 550 / MUET Band 4 or equivalent (waived for English-speaking nation nationals).

📑 Required Documents

  • 📄

  • 📝

  • 📋

Campus Life & Facilities

🏠 Accommodation

UPM offers on-campus student apartments and dormitories with basic amenities. Off-campus housing in Serdang and nearby Putrajaya is also affordable and well-connected.

🍽️ Dining & Culture

Multiple canteens, cafes, and international food courts on campus. The campus hosts cultural festivals, clubs, sports events, and international student gatherings throughout the year.

🏋️ Sports & Recreation

Olympic-sized swimming pool, sports complex, gym, tennis courts, football fields, and an indoor sports arena. Active recreation clubs and adventure groups.

📚 Libraries

The main library and 5 faculty-specific libraries hold over 2 million volumes of books, journals, and digital resources. 24/7 study spaces available.

🔬 Research Facilities

UPM has research farms, a biosafety level 3 lab, food processing pilot plants, an offshore marine research station, and an advanced science park.

🚌 Location & Transport

Located near Putrajaya and Kuala Lumpur. Free campus shuttle buses. Easy access via KTM train to KL Sentral. Nearby airports: KLIA & KLIA2.

Why International Students Choose UPM

UPM offers globally recognized degrees at a fraction of the cost compared to universities in the UK, USA, or Australia — making quality education accessible.

Malaysia is known for its warm hospitality. The multi-ethnic society is welcoming to international students, with English widely spoken on campus and in cities.

An internationally recognized degree opens doors to careers globally. UPM graduates are employed worldwide in agriculture, biotech, engineering, and business sectors.
