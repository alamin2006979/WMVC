Why Study in the UK?

The UK combines centuries of academic excellence with modern innovation. A UK degree is a global passport to career success.

🎓 World-Ranked Universities

The UK has 4 universities in the global top 10 and over 30 in the top 200. Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, and LSE are recognized worldwide as centers of academic excellence.

📜 2-Year Graduate Route Visa

The UK Graduate Route allows international students to stay and work in the UK for up to 2 years after graduation — no job offer required. A fantastic opportunity to launch your global career.

⏱️ Faster Degree Completion

UK undergraduate degrees are typically 3 years (vs. 4 in the US) and most master's programs are 1 year — saving you time and money while still delivering a world-class education.

🌍 Global Career Launchpad

UK degrees carry global weight. Employers in finance (City of London), tech, creative industries, and research actively recruit UK graduates worldwide.

🎭 Rich Cultural Heritage

Study in the birthplace of Shakespeare, the Beatles, and world-changing scientific discoveries. Experience historic campuses, diverse cities, and a vibrant multicultural society.

🔬 Research Excellence

The UK produces 14% of the world's most cited research publications. From physics to humanities, UK universities lead global research across disciplines.

Student Visa (Student Route) Process

1

Receive CAS from Your University

After acceptance, your university will issue a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) — your key document for the visa application.

2

Prepare Your Documents

Gather: valid passport, CAS, proof of English (IELTS UKVI 6.0+), proof of funds (£1,334/month living + tuition), TB test certificate.

3

Apply Online for Student Route Visa

Complete the online application on the UK government website. Pay the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) and visa fee.

4

Attend Biometric Appointment

Book and attend a biometric appointment at the UK Visa Application Centre in Dhaka. Provide your passport and documents.

5

Receive Visa & Travel

Once approved, receive your vignette visa in your passport and travel to the UK before your course start date.

📋 Student Route Visa Requirements

  • CAS from a licensed Student Route sponsor
  • Valid passport
  • Proof of English: IELTS UKVI 6.0+ / PTE Academic UKVI 59+
  • Proof of funds: £1,334/month (London) / £1,023/month (outside London)
  • Tuberculosis test certificate
  • Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) payment
  • Academic certificates and transcripts
  • Passport-size photographs

✅ Graduate Route — 2 Years to Work

After completing your degree, you can stay in the UK for up to 2 years under the Graduate Route — no job offer needed. Use this time to work, job-hunt, or explore career options in the UK.
