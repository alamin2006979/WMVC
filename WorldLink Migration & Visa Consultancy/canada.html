Why International Students Choose Canada

Canada consistently ranks as one of the best countries for education, safety, and quality of life.

🎓 Globally Recognized Education

Canadian universities rank among the world's best. Degrees are recognized globally and valued by employers.

🗺️ Clear PR Pathway

Canada's Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) allows graduates to work up to 3 years — a direct pathway to Permanent Residency through Express Entry.

💰 Affordable Education

Canadian tuition is significantly lower than the US or UK for comparable quality. Many scholarships available.

🌍 Multicultural & Welcoming

Canada is built on immigration. Over 800,000 international students choose Canada each year for its hospitality and diversity.

💼 Part-Time Work During Studies

Work up to 20 hours/week during studies and full-time during breaks — helping fund your education while gaining experience.

🏡 High Standard of Living

Universal healthcare, world-class cities, and stunning natural landscapes make Canada ideal for students and families.

Study Permit (Student Visa) Process

1

Receive Letter of Acceptance (LOA)

Apply to a Canadian Designated Learning Institution (DLI) and receive your Letter of Acceptance before proceeding.

2

Prepare Study Permit Documents

Gather: LOA, valid passport, proof of funds (CAD 20,000+/yr), police clearance, biometric photos, medical exam.

3

Submit Online via IRCC

Apply through the IRCC portal. Ensure all documents are in English or French with certified translations.

4

Biometrics & Medical Exam

Schedule biometrics at the VAC in Dhaka and complete a medical exam at an approved panel physician.

5

Receive Approval & Travel

Once approved, receive your Port of Entry (POE) Letter and travel to Canada to begin your studies.

📋 Study Permit Requirements

  • Letter of Acceptance from a DLI
  • Valid passport
  • Proof of funds: CAD 20,635/year + tuition
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Immigration medical examination (IME)
  • Biometrics at VAC
  • English test: IELTS 6.0+ / TOEFL 80+
  • Proof of ties to Bangladesh

✅ Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)

Graduate from a Canadian DLI and receive a work permit for up to 3 years — gaining Canadian work experience that counts toward Permanent Residency.
