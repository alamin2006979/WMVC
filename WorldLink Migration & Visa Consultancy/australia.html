Why Study in Australia?

Australia combines high-quality education with an excellent quality of life, making it a top choice for Bangladeshi and international students alike.

🎓 Globally Recognized Degrees

Australian universities rank among the world's best. Degrees are accredited, internationally recognized, and highly valued by employers globally.

🌏 Post-Study Work Visa (PSW)

Graduate with a 2–4 year Post-Study Work Visa. Work in Australia after graduation and gain international experience — a pathway to permanent residency.

🏥 World-Class Healthcare

Australia has one of the world's best healthcare systems. International students on a valid visa are covered by Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC).

🌊 Stunning Quality of Life

From Sydney's harbor to Melbourne's coffee culture and Perth's beaches — Australia offers a safe, welcoming, and vibrant lifestyle for students.

💼 Part-Time Work Rights

Student visa holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during studies. This helps cover living costs while gaining valuable work experience.

🤝 PR Pathway for Graduates

Australia's Points-Tested PR system rewards graduates with skills in demand. An Australian degree adds significant points to your PR application.

Subclass 500 Student Visa Process

1

Receive Your CoE

Apply to an Australian university and receive a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) once accepted. This is required for your visa application.

2

Prepare Your Documents

Gather: valid passport, CoE, proof of English (IELTS/PTE), financial evidence, health insurance (OSHC), and GTE statement.

3

Complete Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) Statement

Write a personal statement explaining your intention to study in Australia and return home after your studies.

4

Apply Online via ImmiAccount

Submit your Subclass 500 visa application through the Australian Department of Home Affairs ImmiAccount portal.

5

Health Check & Biometrics

Complete a medical examination at an approved panel clinic and provide biometrics as part of the process.

📋 Subclass 500 Requirements

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from an Australian school
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the entire visa duration
  • English proficiency: IELTS 6.0 (5.5 each band) or PTE 50+
  • GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) statement
  • Proof of funds: AUD 21,041/year + tuition + travel
  • Passport and photograph
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Complete health examinations

✅ OSHC (Health Insurance)

All international students must have OSHC for the duration of their visa. We help arrange this as part of your visa package.
